WoW Forever Class Abilities

Every trainable ability for the nine classes in World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic 1.60 beta), sorted by role, plus a recommended keyboard and mouse layout for each class. Costs, damage and healing shown are the highest rank's; the level is where the first rank is trained (or the earliest a talent can be taken).

Ability data and icons from Wowhead's Forever database (beta build, October 2026). Role categories and keybind placement are editorial suggestions.
